REDEMPTION STANDARDS & FORMS

USD • 31 CFR PART 100 100% REPLACEMENT

United States Dollar Damaged Note Protocol

Bureau of Engraving and Printing (BEP) Mutilated Currency Division. If >50% remains with legible serial, full face value is honored.

EUR • ECB DECISION 2013/10 OFFICIAL EUROSYSTEM

Eurozone Mutilated Banknote Redemption

Exchangeable at any National Central Bank (Bundesbank, Banque de France, etc.) when presenting >50% of the surface area.

GBP • BANK OF ENGLAND POLYMER & PAPER

UK Damaged Banknote Section

Bank of England Debden facility reimburses full value for torn polymer £5, £10, £20, and £50 notes when >50% is verified.

JMD • BANK OF JAMAICA ACT SEC 28 POLYMER SERIES

Jamaican Dollar Salvage Standard

Redeemable at BOJ Nethersole Place, Kingston or via commercial bank remittance for genuine polymer fragments exceeding 50%.

JPY • BANK OF JAPAN TWO-TIER RATIO

Japan Banknote Exchange Standard

≥ 2/3 (66.7%) remaining = 100% face value. Between 2/5 (40.0%) and 2/3 = 50% face value. Under 2/5 = zero value.
